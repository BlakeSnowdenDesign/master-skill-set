# Harbour View Cottages

## Arrive easy. Settle in slowly.

At the quiet southern tip of Harbour Island, two private cottages offer space to slow down and let the island unfold around you.

Set across 0.7 acres of tropical gardens, Harbour View feels comfortably removed from the rush, while Pink Sands Beach and the Briland Club Marina are just a short walk away. Come by boat, spend the afternoon on the water, or stay close to home and let the day take its time.

## A place to feel at home

Each 1,400-square-foot cottage is light, open, and easy to settle into, with natural textures and soft Bahamian color. There’s room to gather over a long meal, stretch out after the beach, or simply enjoy a quiet morning before the island wakes.

The primary bedroom has a king bed, en-suite bath, and closet. In the second bedroom, twin beds can be made up as a king, with an en-suite bath of its own. A modern kitchen, comfortable living room, and generous dining area make it easy to settle into your own rhythm.

More than 700 square feet of deck space extends each cottage outdoors. Take breakfast in the open air, linger over lunch, or pour something cold as the light softens over the marina.

## The water, just beyond the garden

The grounds invite you to take your time: a swim in the pool, a shaded afternoon in the cabana, a slow walk along the garden paths.

For days on the water, the private 80-foot dock has 30, 50, and 100 AMP service and can welcome boats up to 70 feet. Snorkel gear, kayaks, and paddleboards are ready for exploring the turquoise bay. Pink Sands Beach is a short stroll away, with the Briland Club Marina nearby.

## Let Harbour Island unfold

Some days call for the beach. Others are best spent on the water, in the garden, or on the deck with nowhere else to be. Harbour View Cottages offer a private, welcoming place to return to—and the freedom to make the island your own.
