**HIGH TIDE**

**Elevated Island Living**

**High Tide sits slightly above its sister cottage, creating an airy, elevated perspective across Harbour Island's western shore. Designed in the relaxed, sophisticated spirit of Briland, this two-bedroom retreat pairs light-filled interiors with generous outdoor living and beautiful views toward the harbour.**

Inside, approximately 1,400 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space feels bright, open and unmistakably Bahamian. A spacious king suite offers a private en-suite bath and closet, while the second bedroom features twin beds that can be converted to a king, also with its own en-suite bath and storage. A fully equipped kitchen, dining area and comfortable living room make the cottage equally suited to slow mornings, family dinners and evenings spent at home after a day on the water. The cottages' published floor plans are described as essentially identical. Home Rentals

Large outdoor decks extend the cottage into the landscape, creating places to have coffee in the morning, read beneath the palms, or settle in with a cocktail as the afternoon light moves across the bay.

Current Harbour View marketing specifically describes High Tide as the **upper-elevation cottage**, positioned on a coral ridge with a wraparound ocean-view deck; that source also references vaulted ceilings and an outdoor shower connected with the primary suite. I would confirm those details with the owner before making them prominent website claims. Harbour View Cottages
