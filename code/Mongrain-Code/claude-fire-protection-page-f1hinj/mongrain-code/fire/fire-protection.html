Intumescent coatings

Multi-layer protective paint · exposed steel

A specialty paint applied in multiple layers. Under heat it forms a protective barrier, giving exposed steel beams up to three hours of fire resistance while the structure stays visible. One of Mongrain’s specialties.

  • Up to 3 h fire resistance
  • Steel stays visible
  • Largest project in Canada

Fibrous materials · shotcrete · gypsum

Fibrous materials, shotcrete and gypsum are selected to the project specification. They raise the fire resistance of structural elements, keeping them below failure temperature so the building preserves its bearing capacity.

  • Selected per specification

Thermal barriers

Coating over spray foam insulation

A specialized coating applied over spray foam insulation. It isolates the foam from heat and keeps it from igniting, so insulation and fire safety work together in the same assembly.

  • Over spray foam

  • Prevents ignition

Part of the
whole envelope.

Fire protection is planned with the rest of the building envelope, from the first 3D model to the final compliance check, by the same team that builds the walls and roof around it.

  1. 3D design mapping

    Map the elements to protect and how they meet the rest of the envelope, with the design team, before work begins.

  2. Material compatibility

    Confirm each fire product suits its substrate and the adjacent envelope layers it touches.

  3. Application

    Apply each system to the project specification: coatings in layers, fireproofing to thickness, walls and doors as rated.

  4. Compliance verification

    Check the installed work against the specification before handover.
