# Direct collaboration. Thoughtful design.

Snowden Design is Blake Snowden’s independent studio in Denver, Colorado.

When you work with Snowden Design, you work with Blake: the person who listens to the brief, designs the pages, builds the site and answers the email afterward. Nothing gets lost between departments because there are none.

## Art and technology at one desk

Blake’s work moves between website design and development, UX, brand and graphic design, illustration, 3D rendering, photography and video. Keeping those disciplines together means the visual idea survives the trip from first sketch to live site.

Every project starts with strategy: what the business needs, who it needs to reach and what will help them decide. Design, build and content follow from that, along with SEO, GEO and a plan for how the site keeps working after launch.
