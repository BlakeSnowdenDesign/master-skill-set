## Creative direction. Engineered in motion.

Lime Tree Hospitality · Guest Rooms, Volume 01

For Lime Tree Hospitality, I created a cinematic digital editorial that pairs refined visual storytelling with highly technical video production. Using advanced video and AI-assisted editing techniques, I built precise technical overlays, dimensional motion, and smooth rotational zooms to reveal the design details with clarity and impact. The result is a polished, immersive film that feels as thoughtfully engineered as it is beautiful.

The goal: turn a deep hospitality portfolio into something people could browse as naturally as a printed book—without losing the specificity of each destination or the material detail of the furniture.

Approach

### The editorial idea

Rather than treating the collection as a product list, the lookbook begins with the places the furniture serves. A quiet, tactile visual language gives the photography room to lead; a contents rail and page-by-page navigation let visitors move between destinations without losing their place.

Each property pairs a full-bleed room image with a carefully sequenced photographic strip and a walkthrough film. The result keeps the breadth of the collection visible while giving individual spaces their own pace.
