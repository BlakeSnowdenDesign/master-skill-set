## Native section: SEO & GEO — Two disciplines

## Related, but not the same job

### Search engine optimization

Helping search engines crawl, index and rank the right pages for the right searches.

- Technical  Crawlability, page speed, mobile behavior, redirects and indexing.

- Local  Accurate business details, service areas and a Google Business Profile that matches the site.

- On-page  Titles, headings and copy written around what people actually search for.

### Generative engine optimization

Helping AI-assisted search tools describe your business accurately when they summarize it.

- Clarity  Plain statements of who you serve, what you do and where.

- Structure  Headings, lists and structured data that make facts easy to extract.

- Consistency  The same facts across your site, profiles and listings.

## Native section: SEO & GEO — Audits and measurement

## A practical loop from question to improvement.

An audit reviews how pages are crawled and indexed, whether each has a clear purpose, and where content is thin, duplicated or hard to follow. It can also surface practical fixes to titles and descriptions, mobile performance, canonical URLs, image descriptions, internal links and structured data where it fits. The useful output is a prioritized set of fixes—not a score dressed up as a strategy.

Then use tools such as Google Search Console and your site analytics to understand which queries surface your pages, where visitors arrive, and what they do next. Traffic and engagement need context: a spike is a question to explore, not proof of business impact.

GEO is an emerging set of practices, not a switch or a guaranteed citation. Clear, accurate, well-structured information gives search systems a better chance of understanding your work. Results still depend on systems Snowden Design does not control.

## Native section: SEO & GEO — Questions

## Before you decide

Can you guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No. Search engines and AI systems make their own decisions. The work improves the conditions that make a site easier to crawl, understand and trust; outcomes are measured honestly rather than promised.

Does a Wix Studio site work for SEO?

Yes. Wix Studio supports page titles, descriptions, structured data, redirects and clean URLs. Most issues come from structure and content, not the platform.

Is GEO a replacement for SEO?

No. Generative engine optimization builds on the same foundations: clear, accurate, well-structured pages. It adds attention to how your expertise is stated so it can be summarized correctly.
