## Native section: 3D — Where 3D helps

## When 3D makes an idea clearer

Dimension is useful when it removes a paragraph of explanation. It is a visual tool, not a substitute for engineering, permitting or manufacturing review.

-

### Illustrative rendering

Stills of a product, space or building that does not exist yet, or cannot be photographed the way you need.

-

### Dimensional storytelling

Exploded views and assemblies that show how parts relate, so a complex idea reads at a glance.

-

### Interactive exploration

Lightweight web scenes visitors can rotate and inspect, embedded where the explanation happens.
