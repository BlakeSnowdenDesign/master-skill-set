08 / How it works

A clear next step, then another.

Start with what is happening now. The plan follows the business—not a pre-set marketing calendar.

  1. Step 01

    Review

    Look at the website, current marketing and the questions the business needs to answer.

  2. Step 02

    Prioritize

    Agree what matters now, what can wait, and a practical scope for the work.

  3. Step 03

    Improve

    Make the updates, publish useful content and connect each piece to the wider plan.

  4. Step 04

    Refine

    Review available performance information and use it to shape the next set of priorities.
