## Page priorities and proof

- **Home:** Denver web design. Summarize website design and development, Wix
  Studio, UX and branding, then link to the relevant service pages.
- **Work:** actual project examples, including Edge Internet. Use factual
  descriptions of Blake's role, scope and deliverables. Outcomes need evidence.
- **Services:** explain web/UX/branding/video and strategy deliverables. Cover
  related query variants in one useful section rather than creating duplicates.
- **3D & Interactive:** demonstrate 3D design, modeling, rendering and illustrative
  architectural visualization with MONGRAIN. Do not describe it as an as-built
  BIM model or invent engineering certifications.
- **SEO & GEO:** explain technical SEO, local search, content and audits, then
  define generative engine optimization in plain language. Explain that AI
  mentions and rankings cannot be guaranteed.
- **Website Care & Growth:** define ongoing care, reporting, campaign/social
  management, client responsibilities, and what costs extra. Do not publish
  invented pricing, ad budgets, lead counts or response-time promises.
- **About:** establish Blake's expertise and the studio's actual identity.
  Do not use “agency” to imply a team that has not been confirmed.
- **Contact:** clear inquiry path, correct public phone and service context;
  no need to compete with Home for all of its target phrases.

## On-page and GEO practices

1. Give every page one clear native H1 and a unique title and description.
2. Write real service explanations and descriptive links in native Wix text.
   Do not rely on text inside an iframe for the parent page's search visibility.
3. Use truthful Organization/Person information consistently. Do not add fake
   reviews, ratings, street addresses or social profiles to structured data.
4. Put a concise answer next to each relevant question. Attribute real project
   work and distinguish illustrative demonstrations from measured results.
5. Write image alternatives to describe the actual image. Do not attach the
   same “Denver web design” alt text to unrelated images.
6. Keep mobile layouts usable, imagery appropriately sized, links working,
   heading order logical, and text accessible. Check live Core Web Vitals when
   field data is available; do not claim a score without measuring.
7. Verify the chosen domain, canonical URLs, sitemap, indexing settings and any
   redirects. The Replit development preview remains noindex.
8. Do not add meta-keywords, hidden keyword lists, duplicate neighborhood pages,
   or unsupported promises about PageRank, first place or AI citations.
9. Search-crawler access and model-training access are separate choices.
   Neither creates a guarantee that an AI system will cite the business.

## Validate after launch

Establish a baseline in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools. Review
queries, impressions, clicks, click-through rate, landing pages and average
position by page. GA4 can measure agreed inquiry events after consent and event
configuration are verified. Keep the 50-phrase list as the hypothesis; revise
priorities using actual queries and qualified inquiries, not only exact-match ranks.

Log any observed AI referrals or mentions with their date and source; distinguish
observations from attribution claims. Do not present a few manual AI responses as
a stable ranking metric.
