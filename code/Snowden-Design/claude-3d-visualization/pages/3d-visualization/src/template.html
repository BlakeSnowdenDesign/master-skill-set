3D modeling services

What can a 3D model do for your site?

Explain a product or a building in seconds instead of paragraphs, and keep visitors on the page longer while they explore it.

  • 3D modeling

    Buildings, assemblies and products modeled from CAD, drawings, PDFs or photos, simplified so they read clearly on screen.

  • Exploded assembly animation

    Layers that separate, label themselves and reassemble, so the order of a system makes sense at a glance.

  • Interactive web 3D

    Real-time Three.js and WebGL scenes with orbit, zoom, click-to-isolate and guided tours, not a flat video.

  • Rendering and materials

    Distinct material colors, soft shadows and clean lighting that match your brand palette without losing technical clarity.

  • Wix-ready delivery

    Each model ships as one self-contained HTML file you paste into Wix. No apps, no Velo, no monthly 3D-viewer fees.

  • Built to be found

    Models arrive with SEO and GEO copy, alt text and schema for the page around them, so the 3D work also brings in search traffic.
