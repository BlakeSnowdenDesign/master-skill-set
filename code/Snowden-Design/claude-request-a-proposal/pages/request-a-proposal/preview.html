Before you send it

What's in a Snowden Design proposal?

A written plan with the pages or deliverables, the timeline, a fixed price or price range, what I need from you, and the first step to launch.

Do I need an exact budget?

No. A range is enough. It tells me whether to plan a focused launch or a larger build, and the proposal explains what fits inside it.
