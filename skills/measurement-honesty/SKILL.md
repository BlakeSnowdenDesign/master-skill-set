# Measurement and submission honesty

**Evidence: C16, U02/U04; also C09/S09/S17 for local asset restrictions.** ChatGPT recommendations are not proof analytics is installed, consent is obtained or events reach a service.

1. Agree conversion goals, platform, privacy requirements and authorized scope before configuring tracking.
2. Use intentional actions with fixed non-identifying values; exclude names, emails, free text, sensitive URLs, secrets and uploaded customer content.
3. Tracking must be optional and non-blocking. It must not break navigation, form use or downloads.
4. A copied HTML embed does not inherit its development environment's analytics. Verify the actual Wix/parent measurement setup separately.
5. A local enquiry-brief download or copy is not a submitted lead. Require a verified endpoint and successful response before displaying “sent”.
6. Report event definitions, date, data source and limits; no invented traffic, conversion rates, client results or live status.
7. Preserve all exact-image dashboard restrictions and illustrative disclosures. Do not turn source graphics into simulated live metrics.

Read [event/state example](references/events.md). This module installs nothing and authorizes no integration or external data transfer.
