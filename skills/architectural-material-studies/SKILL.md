# Architectural material studies

**Evidence: C15, U03/U04.** Secondary ChatGPT report. The original MONGRAIN skill and approved specimen were not included or inspected. This is a proposed checklist, not a copied or verified source skill.

1. Retrieve the approved specimen and project instructions from the [inventory](../../REPOSITORY-INVENTORY.md). Do not invent an “approved” appearance from the summary.
2. Use a consistent projection and light direction. The report suggests upper-left light, pale upper faces, darker sides, fine leading highlight and contact shadows.
3. Make section boundaries clear before texture. Distinguish coating, expanded char, spray texture, insulation and board/barrier rather than using one generic material fill.
4. When authorized, cut back protection to reveal structure; keep labels outside the assembly and leaders clear.
5. Prefix SVG IDs per scene; verify gradients, patterns, filters and URL references. Keep a stable viewBox, label margins and intentional filter bounds.
6. Add truthful illustrative/not-to-scale disclosure. No diagram establishes a tested rating, code compliance, vendor claim or actual installed assembly.
7. Check geometry, labels, leader contrast and filter clipping at desktop and narrow phone widths.
